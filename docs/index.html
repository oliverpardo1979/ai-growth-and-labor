Year 0

Negative years show the pre-RSI balanced-growth reference. RSI is activated unexpectedly at year 0; the stocks of capital and AI efficiency remain continuous, while choices can jump. The years are model time, not calendar forecasts.

The four economies have different initial capital stocks, chosen to place each on its pre-RSI balanced-growth path. They share the other parameters within each exercise. A point at year 500 is a simulated observation, not necessarily a long-run limit.
